# Experimental-sequence results

Generated with `python examples/generate_sequence_results.py` from the
illustrative 87Rb D2 sequence. The timeline shows finite laser ramps, coil and
eddy-current decay, field settling, molasses, and release. The timing scans use
an effective two-level damping/recoil proxy, not a coherent PGC temperature or
an experimentally optimized recipe. Mains amplitude is intentionally zero
until supplied from a measurement.
