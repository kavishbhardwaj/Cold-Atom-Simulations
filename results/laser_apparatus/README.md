# Six-beam apparatus results

Generated by `python examples/generate_six_beam_apparatus_results.py`.
`beam_diagnostics.csv` gives each physical beam's power, elliptical waists,
direction, and sigma-/pi/sigma+ fractions relative to laboratory z. Figures show
the three counterpropagating pairs and controlled one-at-a-time power, QWP, and
pointing perturbations. The force scan uses the effective MOT model, not the
coherent PGC solver; see `docs/six_beam_apparatus.md` for fidelity limits.
